XeeTok Account & Data Deletion

XeeTok users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the XeeTok app.
  2. Sign in to your XeeTok account.
  3. Open your Profile or account settings.
  4. Select the Account Deletion option.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to submit your deletion request.

If you cannot access your XeeTok account or the in-app deletion option, you can request account deletion by emailing: xeetok@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your XeeTok account where possible, so that we can verify account ownership.

Data that may be deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, data associated with the account may be deleted or anonymized, including:

  • Account and profile information
  • Profile photo or avatar information
  • User-generated content associated with the account, where applicable
  • Social connections and activity associated with the account
  • Messages and other account-related information, where applicable

Data that may be retained

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, financial record-keeping, or enforcement purposes.

Information that is retained will only be kept for as long as necessary for those purposes and will then be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Contact

If you have questions about account or data deletion, contact: xeetok@gmail.com
