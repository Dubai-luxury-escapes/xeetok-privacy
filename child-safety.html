XeeTok Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 7, 2026

XeeTok has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Prohibited conduct and content

XeeTok prohibits any content, communication, account activity, or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, grooms, traffics, sextorts, or otherwise endangers a child. Users must not create, upload, request, distribute, promote, store, or share CSAM or content that facilitates CSAE.

2. Reporting child-safety concerns

Users can report concerning users, messages, rooms, videos, or other user-generated content through XeeTok's in-app reporting mechanisms. Reports involving suspected child exploitation are treated as high-priority safety matters. If a child is in immediate danger, users should contact local emergency services or law enforcement.

3. Enforcement and removal

When XeeTok obtains actual knowledge of content or conduct that violates these standards, we may remove the content, restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant information where legally required, and take other appropriate safety actions. Confirmed CSAM is handled in accordance with applicable law.

4. Reporting to authorities

XeeTok is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. Where required by applicable law, confirmed CSAM and related information are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional or national authority.

5. Cooperation and prevention

XeeTok may cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities and continually reviews its moderation, reporting, blocking, and account-enforcement measures to reduce child-safety risks.

6. Child Safety Point of Contact

Google Play and competent child-safety or law-enforcement authorities may contact XeeTok regarding CSAE/CSAM prevention, enforcement, review procedures, or urgent safety matters at xeetok@gmail.com.

7. Scope

These standards apply to XeeTok users and all user-generated content and communications made available through XeeTok. They supplement XeeTok's other published policies and applicable legal obligations.
