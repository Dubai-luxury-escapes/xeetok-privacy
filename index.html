XeeTok Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

XeeTok ("XeeTok", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use the XeeTok mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use XeeTok, we may collect information such as:

  • Account information, including user ID, username, display name, profile information, and authentication information.
  • Profile photos, avatars, and other content you choose to upload.
  • User-generated content, including videos, images, comments, room messages, and other content submitted through XeeTok.
  • Private messaging information necessary to provide messaging features.
  • Voice and audio data when you participate in voice rooms or other audio features.
  • Social activity such as follows, friendships, gifts, reactions, reports, blocks, and room participation.
  • Purchase and transaction information related to virtual coins, gifts, and other in-app features.
  • Technical information such as device information, app version, diagnostics, crash information, and security-related information.
  • Information you provide when contacting support or submitting reports.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve XeeTok.
  • Create and manage user accounts and profiles.
  • Provide voice rooms, messaging, social, video, gifting, wallet, and other app features.
  • Process and verify eligible in-app purchases and virtual-item transactions.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, unauthorized activity, and security incidents.
  • Enforce our rules and moderate user-generated content.
  • Investigate reports and protect users and the XeeTok community.
  • Provide customer support and important service communications.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Permissions and Device Access

XeeTok may request device permissions when required for a feature, such as microphone access for voice features and access needed to select, capture, or upload media. Permissions are used only for the relevant app functionality and can generally be managed through your device settings.

4. User-Generated Content and Moderation

XeeTok includes user-generated and social content features. We may process content and related information to operate reporting, blocking, safety, moderation, fraud-prevention, and enforcement systems. Content that violates app rules or applicable requirements may be restricted or removed, and accounts may be limited, suspended, or terminated where appropriate.

5. Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers to operate XeeTok, including cloud infrastructure, database and storage services, authentication services, real-time voice infrastructure, payment processing, crash diagnostics, and other technical services.

These may include services provided by Google, Supabase, and LiveKit, where applicable. Such providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own applicable privacy and security terms.

6. Payments and Virtual Items

XeeTok may offer virtual coins, gifts, or other digital items. Eligible purchases made through Google Play are processed using Google Play's billing services. XeeTok does not receive or store your complete payment-card details from Google Play.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate XeeTok, protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with legal obligations, or respond to valid legal requests.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information. However, no internet-based service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide XeeTok, maintain security and transaction integrity, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request account deletion using the account deletion functionality provided by XeeTok where available, or by contacting us. Account deletion may result in deletion or anonymization of associated personal information, subject to information that must be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, transaction, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

XeeTok is not intended for children under the minimum age permitted for the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable requirements. If we learn that such information has been collected improperly, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

12. International Processing

Because XeeTok uses online infrastructure and service providers, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, subject to applicable legal requirements and safeguards.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as XeeTok develops or as legal, technical, or operational requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
